## Page text

**Latest version:** https://playdeezgames.github.io/blue-balls-of-splorr/

**Blue Balls of SPLORR!!** is a Metaphor about keeping it up.

Blue balls fall. Tap one to bop it back into the air, away from where you hit it. Another drops in every few seconds, and sooner each time. When one touches the floor, it's over. There is no win, only how long you lasted.

- Tap or click a ball to bop it. Aim for its side to send it the other way.
- A red mark at the top shows where the next one is coming in.
- Sign the online board with your longest time held.

### How to play

Mouse or touch. Enter or Space starts and restarts; Escape goes back.

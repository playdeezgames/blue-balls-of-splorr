Blue Balls of SPLORR!! has been playable on GitHub Pages since November 23. Today it gets a page here too.

Keep them up. Don't let go. Blue balls fall; tap one to bop it back up, away from where you hit it. Another drops in every few seconds, and sooner each time. When one touches the floor, it's over.

- **No win:** the score is how long you lasted.
- **Aim matters:** hit a ball on its side to send it the other way, and keep them off each other.
- **Online board:** sign it with your longest time held.

The latest version always lives at https://playdeezgames.github.io/blue-balls-of-splorr/ and the itch build can lag behind it.
